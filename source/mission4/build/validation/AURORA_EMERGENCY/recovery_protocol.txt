VAULT-TEC JANUS
EMERGENCY KEY RECOVERY STANDARD

VERSION:
217-A

Once the DH shared secret S has been reconstructed, generate the UTF-8 seed:

VAULT217-JANUS-S

Example:

If S = 999, use:

VAULT217-JANUS-999

The recovery passphrase is the lowercase hexadecimal SHA-256 digest of the seed, with no trailing newline.

JANUS PRIVATE KEY ARCHIVE:

AES-256-CBC
PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256
100000 iterations
OpenSSL salted format
