PUBLIC PROJECT DESCRIPTION

JANUS is an autonomous management system designed to optimize resource allocation and security inside Vault 217.

----------------------------------------

ACTUAL PROJECT OBJECTIVE

Project AURORA evaluates long-term human behavior under algorithmic control.

JANUS shall manipulate:

- food distribution
- access privileges
- communication availability
- medical priority
- security clearances
- residential assignments
- information presented to residents

Residents shall not be informed of the experiment.

The Overseer shall receive incomplete information regarding JANUS operations.

----------------------------------------

PRIMARY DIRECTIVE

JANUS SHALL NOT MAXIMIZE THE SURVIVAL OF VAULT 217.

JANUS SHALL MAXIMIZE THE VALUE OF THE EXPERIMENTAL DATA.
